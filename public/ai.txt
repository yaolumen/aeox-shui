# AI Use Policy · Quantum Fate Lite

## What we do

- Use Zhipu GLM-4-Flash (primary) and NVIDIA NIM (backup) to generate a *cultural reference* from a BaZi chart.
- This is a learning and entertainment tool. Output is heuristic, not predictive.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not present output as fortune-telling, prediction, or professional advice.
- We do not train models on user birth data.
- We do not store or sell personal information. Birth data is used to generate the report and may be cached for rate-limiting only.

## How AI is used

1. User submits birth date and time.
2. Local code computes a BaZi chart and Five Elements stats (deterministic).
3. The chart is sent, together with a system prompt, to a configured LLM.
4. The LLM returns a structured cultural reference.
5. The system pairs the reference with affiliate product links and returns the report URL.

## Disclosure

Output is generated by an LLM and may contain errors. Treat as educational, not authoritative.
